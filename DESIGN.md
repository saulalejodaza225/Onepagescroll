## Brand & Style

This design system expresses an elite, high-touch boutique aesthetic tailored for an architectural and web design studio in Bogotá. It bridges neoclassical editorial prestige with modern computational rigor. 

The stylistic movement is **Neo-Editorial Nocturne with Tactile Skeuomorphic Accents**:
- The emotional posture is mysterious, distinguished, authoritative, and exceptionally polished. It evokes midnight clarity in high-altitude Andean capitals, tailored craftsmanship, and private gallery vernissages.
- The interface lives primarily in a deep, atmospheric dark canvas enriched by illuminated gold accents and tactile, physical affordances on conversion points.
- Interactive controls reject the clinical flatness of standard SaaS, opting instead for physical volume, directional lighting, beveled highlights, and gilded depths that demand to be pressed.

## Colors

The palette—termed *Noche Real*—builds an immersive nocturnal hierarchy punctuated by reflective golden metals and an enigmatic violet counter-accent.

### Core Swatches
- **Medianoche (`#070E1B`):** Canvas base, bottom-most viewports, and deep backdrop planes.
- **Azul Real (`#0B1D33`):** Primary elevated surfaces, panels, navigational docks, and card interiors.
- **Azul Profundo (`#12233B`):** Interactive card hovers, elevated containers, input backgrounds, and segmented group controls.
- **Oro Real (`#F4B63F`):** Core CTA surfaces, high-priority highlights, primary icon strokes, and luminous bevel reflections.
- **Oro Antiguo (`#8A6212`):** Button undersides, inner shade rims, bevel lowlights, and metallic drop borders.
- **Marfil (`#FAF8F3`):** Primary editorial text, hero displays, and prominent quotes.
- **Blanco (`#FFFFFF`):** Specular highlights, active button typography, and acute light reflections.
- **Lino (`#ECE6DA`):** Secondary body copy, warm subtitle accents, and badge backgrounds.
- **Pizarra (`#4E5968`):** Structural wireframes, inactive borders, dividers, and disabled interactive states.
- **Niebla (`#B4BECC`):** Tertiary captions, metadata values, form placeholders, and subtle helper strings.
- **Violeta (`#5E3AA8`):** Selective visual punctuation, exclusive tags, specialized portfolio pill highlights, and radiant atmospheric radial glows behind gold assets.

### Palette Governance
Avoid using Oro Real (`#F4B63F`) for extensive paragraph fills; reserve it strictly for high-focus CTAs, price marks, keymetrics, and active highlights. Medianoche forms the infinite void, layered with Azul Real to structure physical layout elements.

## Typography

The type system balances intellectual gravitas with technical precision through three distinct typographic voices:

1. **Lora (Titles, Brand Statements & Editorial Accents):**
   - Implemented in **SemiBold (600)** for impactful headlines and section titles, providing organic, sculpted serif elegance.
   - Implemented in **Italic (400)** for highlighted keywords, quotes, client testimonials, and studio mottoes.
2. **Poppins (Action Layers, Pricing & Metadata):**
   - Geometric, assertive, and modern. Strictly reserved for uppercase pills, conversion buttons, quantitative metric cards, package tiers, and navigation labels.
3. **Inter (Narrative Body & Functional Forms):**
   - High x-height, neutral, and readable across all dark surfaces. Employed in all long-form paragraphs, form labels, input values, and technical spec sheets.

## Layout & Spacing

The architecture operates on a unified single-page narrative layout running on a responsive 12-column grid system capped at a maximum container width of `1280px`.

### Responsive Rhythm
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, `4rem` outer canvas padding. Section gaps expand to `space-section` (`6rem`) to let high-contrast photography and golden components breathe.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` gutters, `2.5rem` outer canvas margins. Multi-column showcase cards transition into 2-column stacked units.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, `1.25rem` outer margins. Hero displays drop from 64px to 40px, and horizontal pricing cards collapse into full-width individual cards.

### Vertical Section Cadence
Because this is a continuous One-Page narrative, rhythm is dictated by alternating background density:
- Hero, Work Showcase, and Final CTA employ `Medianoche (#070E1B)` ground with ambient violet and gold light cones.
- Services, Process, and Pricing sit within contained sections accented by subtle outline groupings and surface elevations using `Azul Real (#0B1D33)`.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system is tactile, physical, and atmospheric, stepping away from generic fuzzy dropshadows in favor of chiseled surfaces, directional rims, and luminous backdrops.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Backdrop Ground):** `Medianoche (#070E1B)`. Flat, absorption layer with optional radial atmospheric vignettes (`rgba(94, 58, 168, 0.12)`).
- **Tier 1 (Resting Cards & Containers):** `Azul Real (#0B1D33)` paired with a delicate, calibrated perimeter stroke: `1px solid rgba(244, 182, 63, 0.12)`.
- **Tier 2 (Elevated & Active Cards):** `Azul Profundo (#12233B)` overlaid with a dual-stage drop shadow: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(7, 14, 27, 0.8), 0 2px 6px 0 rgba(244, 182, 63, 0.08)`. Border intensifies to `rgba(244, 182, 63, 0.28)`.
- **Tier 3 (Floating Overlays, Modals, Fixed Header Dock):** `rgba(11, 29, 51, 0.85)` with `16px` backdrop-filter blur and an inner hairline: `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(250, 248, 243, 0.1)`.

### 3D Tactile Gold Construction (Buttons & Action Nodes)
Tactile conversion elements are modeled with realistic dimensional presence:
- **Top Chamfer / Specular Rim:** `inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)` simulating polished edge light.
- **Core Body:** Linear gradient angled top-to-bottom: `linear-gradient(180deg, #F9CF7A 0%, #F4B63F 45%, #C98818 100%)`.
- **Base Bevel / Extrusion:** `0 4px 0 0 #8A6212`.
- **Grounded Shadow:** `0 12px 20px 0 rgba(7, 14, 27, 0.5), 0 4px 14px 0 rgba(244, 182, 63, 0.3)`.
- **Active / Pressed State:** Transforms downward by `3px`, bevel compresses to `0 1px 0 0 #8A6212`, shadow softens to reduce apparent altitude.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a balanced, architectural curvature level (`2`):
- **Base Components (Inputs, Chips, Small Buttons):** `0.5rem` (`8px`) radius. Provides a refined, non-aggressive hand-feel.
- **Cards, Panels, and Featured Modules (`rounded-lg`):** `1rem` (`16px`) radius, creating a comfortable frame for editorial mockups and web case studies.
- **Hero Containers & Sticky Floating Menus (`rounded-xl`):** `1.5rem` (`24px`) radius.
- **Pill Accents:** Fully rounded `9999px` radii are reserved exclusively for status badges, service categories, and micro-counters.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary 3D Gold Action:**
  - Typography: Poppins SemiBold, 14px/16px, uppercase tracking (`0.05em`), text rendered in `#070E1B` with subtle contrast shadow (`0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`).
  - Background: Multi-stop gold metallic gradient (`#F9CF7A` through `#C98818`).
  - Construction: Specular top hairline (`rgba(255,255,255,0.6)`), bottom bevel shelf (`#8A6212` 4px solid drop), outer gold aura glow.
  - Hover: Elevation increases via `translateY(-1px)` and expanded golden ambient blur.
  - Active: `translateY(3px)` with bevel reduced to 1px.
- **Secondary Nocturne Button:**
  - Background: `Azul Profundo (#12233B)`.
  - Border: 1px solid `rgba(244, 182, 63, 0.3)`.
  - Typography: Poppins Medium, `#FAF8F3`.
  - Hover: Border switches to `#F4B63F`, background becomes `rgba(244, 182, 63, 0.08)`.

### Cards (Portfolio & Services)
- Background: `Azul Real (#0B1D33)`.
- Border: `1px solid rgba(244, 182, 63, 0.12)`.
- Border Radius: `1rem` (`16px`).
- Padding: `2rem` (`space-xl`).
- Hover Transition: `200ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`. The card translates upward `-4px`, outer border tint increases to `rgba(244, 182, 63, 0.4)`, and an internal top-right specular gradient appears.

### Input Fields & Forms
- Canvas: `Azul Profundo (#12233B)` with inset baseline shadow `inset 0 2px 4px rgba(7, 14, 27, 0.6)`.
- Label: Inter Medium, 14px, `Niebla (#B4BECC)`.
- Placeholder: Inter Regular, 14px, `Pizarra (#4E5968)`.
- Border: `1px solid rgba(78, 89, 104, 0.5)`.
- Focus State: Border snaps to `Oro Real (#F4B63F)`, accompanied by an outer illumination ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(244, 182, 63, 0.2)`. Text renders in `Marfil (#FAF8F3)`.

### Chips & Micro-Badges
- Structure: Rounded-full (`9999px`), padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`.
- Standard Chip: Surface `rgba(18, 35, 59, 0.8)`, border `1px solid rgba(180, 190, 204, 0.2)`, text Poppins 12px Medium in `Niebla (#B4BECC)`.
- Violet Specialty Tag: Surface `rgba(94, 58, 168, 0.15)`, border `1px solid rgba(94, 58, 168, 0.5)`, text Poppins 12px SemiBold in `#FAF8F3`.

### Pricing Tiers
- Tier headers lead with Lora SemiBold titles, followed by Poppins 700 numerical rates styled in `Oro Real (#F4B63F)`.
- Features list uses Inter 14px in `Lino (#ECE6DA)` paired with hand-crafted gold check icons.
- Featured "Recomendado" card includes an elevated top ribbon in `Violeta (#5E3AA8)` with gold embroidery text and an ambient gold container outline.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Base Box: 20x20px square/circle with `0.25rem` radius or circle, background `Azul Profundo (#12233B)`, border `1.5px solid Pizarra (#4E5968)`.
- Checked State: Background transitions to `Oro Real (#F4B63F)`, border becomes `Oro Antiguo (#8A6212)`, inner glyph/dot colored `Medianoche (#070E1B)`.
